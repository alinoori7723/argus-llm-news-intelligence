# Third-party materials

The synthetic showcase dataset in `samples/` and the intelligence workspace's committed run are fictional. They are separate from recorded source fixtures used to test the RSS ingestion domain.

Recorded Federal Reserve RSS fixtures under `src/fixtures/recordedSources/` retain their original source URLs, capture metadata, and payload hashes in neighboring metadata files. The Federal Reserve Board is the source of that material. Inclusion is for parser and provenance regression testing and does not imply endorsement. The repository's MIT license does not grant rights to third-party material beyond those provided by its source.

Dependencies retain their respective licenses. They are installed through the pinned lockfile and are not vendored into this source tree.
